# Coastal Swim Trajectory Profile Design Evolution (Graphics Changelog)

This document tracks the evolution of the **Coastal Swim Trajectory Profile** in `docs/experiments/graphics/kust-zwem-profiel/`.

---

### Version Overview

| Version | Source Files (in `./hist/`) | Key Features & Architectural Changes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **v0** | `ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v0.png` | **Original Hand Sketch by Marc Portier**: Conceptual space-time diagram featuring fixed 750m distance checkpoints and vertically shifted tidal fishbone spines matching swimmer checkpoint arrival times. Three swimmer pace scenarios (blue, green, orange). |
| **v1** | `generate_v1.py`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v1.svg`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v1.png` | **First Digital Vector Prototype**: <br>• 5 checkpoints at 750m intervals (0.0k to 3.0k: Bredene Post 4 ➔ De Haan).<br>• Vertical fishbone spines per checkpoint with hydrodynamic vectors (flood right, ebb left, slack green).<br>• Horizontal green baseline representing the reference swimmer (1.0 m/s cruise pace).<br>• Angled trajectory lines for faster swimmers (blue, rising = earlier) and recreational swimmers (orange, declining = later).<br>• Timing summary cards below each checkpoint.<br>• 100% vector SVG and high-resolution PNG preview. |
| **v2** | `generate_v2.py`<br>`data_to_plot_v2.yaml`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v2.svg`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v2.png` | **Out-and-Back Trajectory Profile (Bredene Post 4 ➔ Vosseslag ➔ Bredene Post 4 • 6.0 km)**: <br>• **Swimmer-Centric Forward Axis**: Reading Left-to-Right is strictly the forward swimming direction across both legs.<br>• **Visual Asymmetry & Inverted Beach Perspective**: Heenweg has clean white background (swimming NE towards NL, matching open-sea view); Terugweg has tinted grey hatched background (`#f8fafc`) to indicate reverse perspective (swimming SW towards FR).<br>• **Current Vector Reversal**: Right-pointing arrow ALWAYS indicates MEESTROOM (tailwind) for the swimmer.<br>• **Isochrones & Isotachs**: Initial prototype with 5 speeds and linear time shifts. |
| **v3** | `generate_v3.py`<br>`data_to_plot_v3.yaml`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v3.svg`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v3.png` | **Dynamic Kinematics, Turnaround Buffer & Minimal Aesthetic**: <br>• **Variable Hydrodynamic Kinematics**: $v_{\text{ground}} = v_{\text{water}} + u(t, s)$ integrated across every 750m leg. Non-linear tidal progression naturally curves isochrones and isotach trajectories.<br>• **Turnaround Buffer Zone ("Niemandsland")**: Turnaround point duplicated into two distinct spines (Vosseslag Arrival vs Vosseslag Departure). Horizontal transition zone with stacked 180° turnaround badge (`↺`). Background shifts from white to grey halfway across the buffer.<br>• **Realistic Tidal Headwind at Turnaround**: Current at Vosseslag is not zero at turnaround; lingering flood requires temporary headwind effort until kentering, accurately illustrated by orange bars pointing left on return departure.<br>• **First-Step Vector Addition Box**: Visual breakdown above leg 0➔1 demonstrating swimmer velocity ($56.8\%$) + current boost ($43.2\%$ "kado") summing exactly to the 750m checkpoint.<br>• **9-Isotach Continuous Fan**: Paces from $0.6\text{ m/s}$ to $1.4\text{ m/s}$ in $0.1\text{ m/s}$ steps with smooth cubic curves and sharp line intersections.<br>• **Alternative Departure Baselines**: Faint dotted green trajectories for departures at -30', -15', +15', +30' from 10:00.<br>• **Minimalist Layout ("Less is More")**: Replaced bulky cards and P4..P1 badges with clean 90° rotated metric chainage ticks (`0.00` to `6.00 km`) and nominal ETA timestamps. Removed personal names from footer. |
| **v4** | `generate_v4.py`<br>`data_to_plot_v4.yaml`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v4.svg`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v4.png` | **Discrete Kinematics (25m Grid), 8 Schalingsbalken & Predictable Triangle Tide Model**: <br>• **Rigorous Numerical Integration on Fine Grid ($\Delta s = 25\text{ m}$)**: Completely eliminated global cubic Bézier spline interpolation across checkpoints. Direct integration of $dt = ds / (v_{\text{water}} + u_{\text{rel}}(t))$ prevents artificial spline oscillations and unnatural trajectory pinching.<br>• **Decoupled Turnaround Corridor**: Inbound leg (0k0 ➔ 3k0) and return leg (3k0 ➔ 6k0) strictly disconnected across the turnaround buffer. Isotachs terminate at Vosseslag Inbound spine and restart at Outbound spine; isochrones connect with straight horizontal bridges bearing clock timestamps.<br>• **8 Alternating Schalingsbalken (Vector Breakdown Cards)**: Explicit vector addition rendered for every single 750m leg. Alternates above (odd legs 1, 3, 5, 7) and below (even legs 2, 4, 6, 8) the graph. Shows exact distance covered through water by swimmer vs pushed/pulled by current in meters and $\text{m/s}$. Tegenstroom leg (Leg 5) shows swimmer exceeding 750m with opposing current subtracted.<br>• **Predictable Triangular Tidal Current Model**: Piecewise linear triangle wave ($\Delta u = 0.20\text{ m/s}$ per 15 min, range $\pm 0.60\text{ m/s}$, slack at 10:45 CEST) providing an exact mental and manual verification baseline.<br>• **Coherent Vertical Spines & Isochrones**: Current staven on spines placed at exact isochrone clock times, directly demonstrating the 7-8 min vertical time shift between spines. Unified departure badges on the left spine and complete labels for all isochrones entering the canvas. |

| **v5** | `generate_v5.py`<br>`data_to_plot_v5.yaml`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v5.svg`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v5.png` | **DIN A3 Landscape Geometry, Soft Envelope Window, Physical Tidal Color Logic & Bottom Ribbon Cards**: <br>• **DIN A3/A4 Landscape Geometry ($\sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$)**: Canvas resized to $1420 \times 1004\text{ px}$, calibrated for printouts and presentations. Vertical scale expanded to $6.55\text{ px/min}$ ($550\text{ px}$ height) focused tightly on the operational core window ($\pm 42\text{ min}$). Horizontal column spacing widened to $dx = 125\text{ px}$.<br>• **Soft Green Starttijd-Venster (Envelope Band)**: Replaced 4 dashed green lines and left badges with a translucent green envelope band (`#10b981`, $8\%$ opacity) representing a $\pm 30\text{ min}$ departure margin that terminates cleanly at the turnaround spines.<br>• **Strict Physical Tidal Current Colors**: Flood current is always blue (`#0284c7`), Ebb current is always purple (`#4338ca`), return leg current staves receive diagonal hatch (`url(#diagonalHatch)`). Completely decoupled from tailwind/headwind direction (Right = $\oplus$, Left = $\ominus$). Eliminated orange for current (orange reserved exclusively for slow swimmer). Eliminated green slack dot (stave length directly indicates magnitude; slack renders as near-zero sliver without clutter). Active nominal stave highlighted with dark outline.<br>• **8 Schalingsbalken Consolidated Below Matrix**: All 8 vector breakdown cards arranged in an uninterrupted ribbon below the graph. Features distinct black vertical tick at exact $750\text{ m}$. Return leg cards tinted light grey (`#f1f5f9`). Compact text formula: `Dw m + Du m (u = ... m/s • dt')`.<br>• **Isochrones & Finish Table**: 10-minute dashed grey isochrones with entering labels rotated along slope and white halo (`paint-order="stroke"`). Dedicated two-column finish table ($v$ and $\Delta t$) on the right margin positioned cleanly above curves.<br>• **Streamlined Header, Simple Banners & Single-Line Legend**: Streamlined perspective banners (`--> ZWEMRICHTING OOST [NL]`, `<-- ZWEMRICHTING WEST [FR]`) and single-line consolidated bottom legend. |
