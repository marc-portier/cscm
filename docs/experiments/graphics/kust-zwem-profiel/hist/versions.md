# Coastal Swim Trajectory Profile Design Evolution (Graphics Changelog)

This document tracks the evolution of the **Coastal Swim Trajectory Profile** in `docs/experiments/graphics/kust-zwem-profiel/`.

| **v3** | `generate_v3.py`<br>`data_to_plot_v3.yaml`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v3.svg`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v3.png` | **Dynamic Kinematics, Turnaround Buffer & Minimal Aesthetic**: <br>• **Variable Hydrodynamic Kinematics**: $v_{\text{ground}} = v_{\text{water}} + u(t, s)$ integrated across every 750m leg. Non-linear tidal progression naturally curves isochrones and isotach trajectories.<br>• **Turnaround Buffer Zone ("Niemandsland")**: Turnaround point duplicated into two distinct spines (Vosseslag Arrival vs Vosseslag Departure). Horizontal transition zone with stacked 180° turnaround badge (`↺`). Background shifts from white to grey halfway across the buffer.<br>• **Realistic Tidal Headwind at Turnaround**: Current at Vosseslag is not zero at turnaround; lingering flood requires temporary headwind effort until kentering, accurately illustrated by orange bars pointing left on return departure.<br>• **First-Step Vector Addition Box**: Visual breakdown above leg 0➔1 demonstrating swimmer velocity ($56.8\%$) + current boost ($43.2\%$ "kado") summing exactly to the 750m checkpoint.<br>• **9-Isotach Continuous Fan**: Paces from $0.6\text{ m/s}$ to $1.4\text{ m/s}$ in $0.1\text{ m/s}$ steps with smooth cubic curves and sharp line intersections.<br>• **Alternative Departure Baselines**: Faint dotted green trajectories for departures at -30', -15', +15', +30' from 10:00.<br>• **Minimalist Layout ("Less is More")**: Replaced bulky cards and P4..P1 badges with clean 90° rotated metric chainage ticks (`0.00` to `6.00 km`) and nominal ETA timestamps. Removed personal names from footer. |
| **v4** | `generate_v4.py`<br>`data_to_plot_v4.yaml`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v4.svg`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v4.png` | **Discrete Kinematics (25m Grid), 8 Schalingsbalken & Predictable Triangle Tide Model**: <br>• **Rigorous Numerical Integration on Fine Grid ($\Delta s = 25\text{ m}$)**: Completely eliminated global cubic Bézier spline interpolation across checkpoints. Direct integration of $dt = ds / (v_{\text{water}} + u_{\text{rel}}(t))$ prevents artificial spline oscillations and unnatural trajectory pinching.<br>• **Decoupled Turnaround Corridor**: Inbound leg (0k0 ➔ 3k0) and return leg (3k0 ➔ 6k0) strictly disconnected across the turnaround buffer. Isotachs terminate at Vosseslag Inbound spine and restart at Outbound spine; isochrones connect with straight horizontal bridges bearing clock timestamps.<br>• **8 Alternating Schalingsbalken (Vector Breakdown Cards)**: Explicit vector addition rendered for every single 750m leg. Alternates above (odd legs 1, 3, 5, 7) and below (even legs 2, 4, 6, 8) the graph. Shows exact distance covered through water by swimmer vs pushed/pulled by current in meters and $\text{m/s}$. Tegenstroom leg (Leg 5) shows swimmer exceeding 750m with opposing current subtracted.<br>• **Predictable Triangular Tidal Current Model**: Piecewise linear triangle wave ($\Delta u = 0.20\text{ m/s}$ per 15 min, range $\pm 0.60\text{ m/s}$, slack at 10:45 CEST) providing an exact mental and manual verification baseline.<br>• **Coherent Vertical Spines & Isochrones**: Current staven on spines placed at exact isochrone clock times, directly demonstrating the 7-8 min vertical time shift between spines. Unified departure badges on the left spine and complete labels for all isochrones entering the canvas. |

| **v5** | `generate_v5.py`<br>`data_to_plot_v5.yaml`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v5.svg`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v5.png` | **DIN A3 Landscape Geometry, Multi-Regime Tide (Headwind on Return), White Cross-Hatching & Calibrated Vector Ribbons**: <br>• **DIN A3/A4 Landscape Geometry ($\sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$) & Canvas Centering**: Canvas at $1420 \times 1004\text{ px}$. Horizontally centered across margins ($x_0 = 105\text{ px}$, $dx = 125\text{ px}$). Vertical plot matrix expanded to $620\text{ px}$ height ($7.38\text{ px/min}$ scale) by lowering ribbon ($y=818$) and legend ($y=890$).<br>• **Multi-Regime Tidal Phase Calibration (Kentering at 10:55 CEST)**: Realistic progression demonstrating all 4 physical hydrodynamic regimes in one swim: Outbound legs 1–4 under strong flood tailwind (`+256m` to `+192m`), Turnaround at 10:34 directly into lingering flood headwind (Leg 5: `u = -0.18 m/s`, `-166m` drag, $916\text{ m}$ effort through water), Kentering at 10:55 on Leg 6 (`u = +0.00 m/s`, exact $750\text{ m}$ swim), and accelerating ebb tailwind home on Legs 7–8 (`+103m`, `+171m`).<br>• **Calibrated Headwind Vector Geometry (Card 5)**: Rescaled bottom ribbon cards to $w_{\text{track}} = 75.0\text{ px}$ ($0.10\text{ px/m}$) so the full $916\text{ m}$ swimmer effort ($91.6\text{ px}$) fits without clipping. The black $750\text{ m}$ target tick mark is placed inside the green bar at $87.0\text{ px}$. The $166\text{ m}$ opposing current overlay ($16.6\text{ px}$) extends from the tick to the bar end with translucent flood blue tint, high-contrast white cross-hatching, and a white leftward arrow pointing back to $750\text{ m}$.<br>• **High-Contrast White Nautical Cross-Hatching (`#whiteCrossHatch`)**: Replaced faint single-angle diagonal hatch with crisp, dense bidirectional white cross-hatching (+45° and -45° at 6px pitch). Clearly distinguishes return leg current staves, schaalbalken, and legend indicators on both print and screen.<br>• **Bidirectional Vertical Density Gradient for Starttijd-Venster**: Translucent emerald band (`#10b981`) rendered with vertical density gradient peaking at 22% opacity along the nominal 10:00 trajectory and fading to near-transparent (2%) at the $\pm 30\text{ min}$ boundaries, bounded by dashed green lines without side vertical borders.<br>• **Comprehensive Isochrone Labeling & Finish Table**: Every 10-minute isochrone entering from spine 0 or the bottom margin is labeled with rotated monospace timestamps and white halo (`paint-order="stroke"`). Checkpoint headers feature `km` prefixes and inline clock icons. Finish table highlights the nominal reference swimmer with bold green ETA (`11:22 ETA`).<br>• **Precise Tidal Current Terminology (`Vloedstroom` & `Ebstroom`)**: Perspective banners and the bottom legend strictly distinguish horizontal tidal currents (`Vloedstroom: heen / terug`, `Ebstroom: heen / terug`) from vertical water level changes. |
| **v6** | `fetch_data_v6.py`<br>`render_v6.py`<br>`data_to_plot_v6.yaml`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v6.svg`<br>`ontwerp-kustzwem-profiel-v6.png` | **Authentic Copernicus Marine (CMEMS) 2D Hydrodynamics & Flemish HIC KiWIS Operational Tide Integration (Saturday 24 Oct 2026)**: <br>• **End-to-End Operational Forecast Pipeline via CSCM Broker**: Automated integration of Copernicus CMEMS 1.5km 2D hydrodynamic forecasts (uo, vo) and HIC KiWIS operational water levels (KT-Last with +7 min phase shift for Bredene).<br>• **Real-World Saturday 24 October 2026 Tidal Window**: High Water at 12:32 CEST (4.70m TAW), Low Water at 18:07 CEST (0.77m TAW). Departure at 10:00 CEST (slack water kentering transitioning into flood).<br>• **Authentic Alongshore Coastal Kinematics**: Discrete 25m numerical integration (`cscm.kinematics.AlongshoreTrajectoryCalculator`) along actual chainage markers (34k500 to 37k500 and back). Outbound leg assisted by nascent flood (+0.03 to +0.22 m/s, 44.7 min to Vosseslag); return leg turns into peak flood (-0.33 to -0.77 m/s opposing current), demonstrating dramatic return drag (149.1 min return, 193.8 min total, 13:13 finish for 1.0 m/s nominal swimmer).<br>• **9-Isotach Velocity Fan & Slower Swimmer Dynamics**: At 0.6 m/s, opposing current severely impedes progress, requiring 375.1 min (16:15 finish, +181 min relative to nominal); 1.4 m/s fast swimmer punches through and finishes at 11:24 (-110 min).<br>• **Proportional Headwind Cards (Schalingsbalken)**: Automatically scales extreme swimmer water distances (up to 3284m effort through water vs -2534m current drag on Leg 8) within compact ribbon cards ($w_{\text{box}} = 115\text{ px}$) with 750m target black tick mark.<br>• **Decoupled Architecture**: Data fetching and domain computations separated into `fetch_data_v6.py` producing a clean, self-contained 173 KB YAML data contract (`data_to_plot_v6.yaml`). Renderer `render_v6.py` operates strictly offline with zero external network or model dependencies.<br>• **Header Tidal Badges & Axis Header Alignment**: Dedicated HW/LW forecast badges in header banner; finish table cleanly aligned with top checkpoint headers ($y = 148$). |
